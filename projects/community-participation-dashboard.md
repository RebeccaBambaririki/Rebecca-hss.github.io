# Community Participation Dashboard (Youth Programs)

## Overview
This project analyzes participation trends in youth programs, focusing on attendance, engagement, and seasonal patterns. The goal is to help community organizations understand how youth interact with programs and identify opportunities to improve access, retention, and impact.

## Why This Matters
Youth programs play a critical role in community development, newcomer integration, and social support. Understanding participation trends helps organizations:
- Allocate resources effectively
- Improve program scheduling
- Strengthen engagement strategies
- Identify gaps in service delivery

## Data Sources (Publicly Available or Simulated)
- Community program attendance logs
- Youth engagement surveys
- Seasonal participation trends
- Public demographic data (Statistics Canada)
- Newcomer youth support reports

## Key Questions
- Which programs have the highest attendance?
- How does participation change across seasons?
- Are newcomer youth participating at similar rates?
- What factors influence attendance (time, location, age group)?
- Which programs show strong retention?

## Methods
1. Collected attendance and participation data.
2. Cleaned and organized data in Excel.
3. Built visualizations (bar charts, line charts, heatmaps).
4. Compared participation across age groups and program types.
5. Identified seasonal patterns and engagement gaps.
6. Developed recommendations for program improvement.

## Sample Insights 
- Participation peaks during summer months.
- Newcomer youth show higher engagement in language-support programs.
- After-school programs have strong retention but lower initial attendance.
- Weekend programs attract mixed age groups.

## Recommendations
- Increase program offerings during high-demand seasons.
- Strengthen newcomer-focused youth programs.
- Improve outreach for low-attendance programs.
- Offer flexible scheduling for working families.
- Use digital tools to track attendance more efficiently.
