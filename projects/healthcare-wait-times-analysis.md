# NL Healthcare Wait Times Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes healthcare wait times across Newfoundland & Labrador, focusing on emergency care, diagnostic imaging, and access to primary care. The goal is to identify bottlenecks, highlight regional disparities, and propose data‑driven recommendations to improve patient access.

## Why This Matters
NL consistently reports some of the longest wait times in Canada. Understanding where delays occur helps health leaders allocate resources, improve scheduling, and strengthen community health outcomes.

## Data Sources (Publicly Available)
- Canadian Institute for Health Information (CIHI)
- NL Health Services wait time reports
- Government of NL health statistics
- Public ER wait time dashboards 

## Key Questions
- Which services have the longest wait times?
- How do wait times differ between regions (St. John’s vs rural areas)?
- Are wait times improving or worsening over the last 5 years?
- What factors contribute to delays (staff shortages, demand spikes, seasonal trends)?
- Which interventions could reduce wait times?

## Methods
1. Collected publicly available wait time data.
2. Cleaned and organized data in Excel.
3. Built visualizations (line charts, bar charts, heatmaps).
4. Compared regional performance.
5. Identified trends and bottlenecks.
6. Developed recommendations based on findings.

## Sample Insights (Example)
- ER wait times peak between 4 PM and 10 PM.
- MRI wait times increased by 12% over the last 3 years.
- Rural areas experience longer delays for specialist appointments.
- Seasonal spikes occur during winter months.

## Recommendations
- Increase staffing during peak ER hours.
- Expand diagnostic imaging capacity.
- Improve triage processes.
- Strengthen virtual care options for rural communities.
- Implement predictive scheduling based on seasonal demand.
