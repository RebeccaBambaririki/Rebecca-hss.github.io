# **CNA Data Analytics Capstone Project — Customer Churn Analysis**

## **Overview**
This capstone project was completed as part of the Data Analytics program at CNA. Working collaboratively with two colleagues, we built an end‑to‑end analytics solution using real-world telecom customer data. The project demonstrates skills in data cleaning, exploration, visualization, predictive modeling, and insight communication.

## **Problem Overview**
Telecom companies face rising customer churn, with many losing 15–25% of customers annually. Churn impacts revenue stability, forecasting accuracy, and customer loyalty. A data‑driven approach is required to understand why customers leave and how to retain them.

- Performed exploratory data analysis

- Designed charts and visuals

- Built and evaluated predictive models

- Wrote insights and recommendations

- Contributed to the final presentation

## **Data & Analytics Approach**
- Data cleaning and preprocessing

- Exploratory data analysis (EDA)

- Feature engineering

- Predictive modeling (Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest)

- Insight generation and recommendations

## **Key Insights**
- Short‑tenure customers churn more frequently

- High monthly charges strongly predict churn

- Poor customer service interactions increase churn risk

- Month‑to‑month contracts are a major churn driver

## **Model Results**
**Best Model: Random Forest**

- Strong accuracy and recall

- Effective at identifying high‑risk customers

- Highlighted top churn predictors

## **Recommendations**
- Offer retention incentives to high‑risk customers

- Improve customer service response times

- Provide flexible contract options

- Support new customers during onboarding

## **Expected Outcomes**
- Reduce churn by 10% within one year

- Improve customer segmentation

- Strengthen decision‑making with clear behavioral insights
