Overview

This project explores the causes of the youth mental health crisis, including social pressures, economic instability, digital exposure, and access barriers to mental health services.

Tools Used

Excel · Power BI · Python · pandas · Jupyter Notebook

Workflow

  • Literature review
  • Data cleaning
  • Trend visualization
  • Risk factor analysis
  • Policy-aligned recommendations

Key Insights

Youth mental health challenges are rising due to social isolation, academic pressure, economic uncertainty, and limited access to early intervention services.

What I Learned

I learned how to combine qualitative and quantitative data to explain complex social issues clearly.
